# Medication and health tasks
<!-- species: all -->

## Giving medication on schedule
Medication should be given at the same time each day and never skipped or doubled up without veterinary advice. Medications for chronic conditions such as thyroid disease are time-sensitive, so they take priority over other tasks. If a dose is missed, contact the veterinarian or pharmacist for guidance rather than guessing. This assistant does not recommend dosages. Always follow the dose on the prescription label and ask the veterinarian about any change.

## Flea, tick and parasite prevention
Flea and tick preventives are usually given monthly, and some are weekly or every few months. Use a product made for the right species, since some dog products are dangerous to cats. Follow the label and ask a veterinarian before combining products.

## Signs that need a veterinarian
Contact a veterinarian promptly for repeated vomiting, not eating for more than a day (sooner for cats), trouble breathing, collapse, blood in stool or urine, straining to urinate, or sudden behavior change.
