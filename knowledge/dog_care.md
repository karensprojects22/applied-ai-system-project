# Dog care
<!-- species: dog -->

## Daily walks and exercise
Most adult dogs need at least 30 minutes of walking or active exercise every day, and many active breeds such as retrievers, collies and huskies need 60 minutes or more. Split exercise into two or more outings when possible. Puppies need shorter, more frequent walks, and senior dogs benefit from gentler, shorter walks. Skipping exercise for several days can lead to restlessness and destructive behavior.
<!-- rec: species=dog category=walk min_minutes=30 -->

## Feeding adult dogs
Adult dogs usually do best with two measured meals a day, spaced about 8 to 12 hours apart. Keep mealtimes consistent. Follow the portion guidance on the food label and adjust with your veterinarian for weight and activity. Fresh water must be available at all times.
<!-- rec: species=dog category=feeding min_minutes=10 -->

## Training and play
Short training sessions of 5 to 15 minutes, once or twice a day, work better than one long session. Mental enrichment such as puzzle feeders, sniff walks and training games tires a dog out as much as physical exercise does.
<!-- rec: species=dog category=enrichment min_minutes=15 -->

## Grooming dogs
Brushing a short-coated dog weekly and a long or double-coated dog several times a week reduces shedding and prevents mats. Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks and check ears for redness or odor. Dental care, such as brushing teeth, is recommended several times a week.
