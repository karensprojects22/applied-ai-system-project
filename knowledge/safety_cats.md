## Foods and plants that are toxic to cats
Lilies are extremely dangerous to cats, and even a small amount of pollen or water from the vase can cause kidney failure. Onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes and raisins, alcohol, and caffeine are also toxic. Never give a cat dog flea products.
