# Feeding and hydration
<!-- species: all -->

## Water
Clean, fresh water should be refilled at least once a day, and more often in hot weather. Wash water bowls regularly, since a dirty bowl grows bacteria. A pet that suddenly drinks much more or much less than usual should be checked by a veterinarian.
<!-- rec: species=all category=feeding min_minutes=5 -->

## Meal consistency
Feed at consistent times and measure portions rather than guessing. Make food changes gradually over 7 to 10 days to avoid stomach upset. Treats should stay under about 10 percent of daily calories.
