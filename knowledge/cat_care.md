# Cat care
<!-- species: cat -->

## Feeding cats
Adult cats usually eat two or more small meals a day. Cats are obligate carnivores and need a complete cat food. Wet food adds hydration. Keep food bowls away from litter boxes and clean them daily. Fresh water should always be available, and many cats drink more from a fountain.
<!-- rec: species=cat category=feeding min_minutes=10 -->

## Play and enrichment for cats
Indoor cats need daily interactive play, ideally two sessions of 10 to 15 minutes, using wand toys or other toys that mimic prey. Play before a meal fits a cat's natural hunt, catch and eat pattern. Rotating toys and providing climbing spaces reduces boredom and stress.
<!-- rec: species=cat category=enrichment min_minutes=15 -->

## Litter box care
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and change the litter fully every one to two weeks. Provide one box per cat plus one extra. A sudden change in litter habits can signal illness and deserves a call to the veterinarian.

## Grooming cats
Short-haired cats benefit from brushing once a week, and long-haired cats need brushing most days to prevent mats and hairballs. Trim claws every few weeks.
