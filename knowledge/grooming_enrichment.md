# Grooming and enrichment basics
<!-- species: all -->

## Brushing and coat care
Regular brushing removes loose hair, spreads natural oils and lets you check the skin for lumps, parasites and irritation. Brush gently and stop if the pet is stressed. Reward calm behavior with a treat.
<!-- rec: species=all category=grooming min_minutes=10 -->

## Mental enrichment
Pets that are bored can develop stress behaviors. Puzzle feeders, rotating toys, short training sessions and new scents provide mental stimulation. A few minutes of focused attention each day is more valuable than long distracted time.
